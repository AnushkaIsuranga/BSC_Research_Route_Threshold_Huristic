## Figure 4.9: `fig4_9_router_overhead`

Why P5 failed: the router's per-query decision cost on EDGAR, by filter breadth (40 pairs each). Lower bar of each pair: median over pairs of each step's per-pair median over 5 timed runs (statistics check, planner estimate, capped count where the band needed one: 30 of 40 section pairs, none elsewhere), stacked with the exact route it then ran; the tick is the router's median end-to-end latency (the stacked medians do not add up to it exactly; about 1 ms is outside the steps). Upper bar: always-exact median latency. The router chose exact on all 160 pairs, so it paid 12-18 ms for the decision and then ran the same query; this is why P5 (router faster than always-exact at year+section and section) did not hold. Exploratory description from the route traces.

Sources: `outputs/edgar_report_inputs_2026-11-01/decision_overhead.json`
